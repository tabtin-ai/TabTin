# Workflow

## 1. Confirm the Customer Context

Ask or infer only from explicit context:

- Customer/account name.
- Meeting or follow-up goal.
- Audience: internal prep, customer-facing summary, sales handoff, or renewal review.
- Allowed sources: mail, documents, tables, prior notes.

## 2. Collect Evidence

Gather recent communication, commitments, open issues, business stage, and known risks.

Keep a source map. If a claim cannot be tied to a source, label it as a hypothesis or `待确认`.

## 3. Draft the Brief

The brief should fit one page unless the user asks for detail:

- Customer situation.
- Recent interactions.
- Current needs.
- Risks/blockers.
- Our commitments.
- Recommended next step.
- Optional email/script draft.

## 4. Confirm Persistence

Before saving customer-sensitive data, show the draft and ask whether it should be written to TabDoc or a customer follow-up table.

Do not create duplicate CRM tables without asking whether a customer table already exists.

## 5. Execute and Report

After confirmation, write the brief, update follow-up records, or draft email. Report what was saved and what remains uncertain.
